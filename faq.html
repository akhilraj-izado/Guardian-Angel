Find answers to common questions about our services, volunteering, donations, and how we care for those in need with compassion and dignity.

Guardian Angel Care Charitable Society is a registered non-profit organization established in 1985, dedicated to providing compassionate care, healthcare, education, rehabilitation, and community support to vulnerable individuals and families.

We offer comprehensive elder care, specialized mental health rehabilitation, education for children, hospital palliative support, and community welfare programmes across Kerala.

Vulnerable seniors, destitute individuals, underprivileged students, patients needing end-of-life care, and families experiencing crises irrespective of caste or creed.

With 100+ dedicated staff, 24/7 medical supervision, hygienic living spaces, healthy meals, and emotional guidance rooted in love and respect.

Visitors and family members are welcome during official visiting hours. Prior confirmation with the administrative office helps us ensure safety and tranquility.

You can fill out the volunteer application form on our website or get in touch directly via our contact number. We accommodate both on-site and remote community roles.
