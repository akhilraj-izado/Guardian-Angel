Our Projects

Projects of Compassion,
Hope & Care

Guardian Angel Care Charitable Society began as a small charitable initiative inspired by compassion for the poor and suffering. Over nearly four decades, the Society has expanded into a multifaceted humanitarian organization serving thousands of individuals across Kerala.

What began with prayer, spiritual preparation, and a dream of service has today become a trusted institution.

Providing a loving shelter, nutritious food, education, and moral guidance to underprivileged and orphaned boys to build a promising future.

Bethsada Geriatric Destitute Home, Vengola
